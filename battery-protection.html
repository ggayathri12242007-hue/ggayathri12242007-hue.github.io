Automatic Battery Overcharge Protection

Project Status: Completed

This project protects a battery from overcharging by automatically disconnecting the charging supply when the voltage reaches a preset limit.

Components Used

  • LM358 Operational Amplifier
  • BC547 Transistor
  • 5V Relay
  • 10kΩ Potentiometer
  • Resistors and LEDs
  • 1N4007 Diode
  • Breadboard and Jumper Wires

Working Principle

LM358 acts as a voltage comparator. It compares the battery voltage with a preset reference voltage. When the voltage reaches the set limit, the relay disconnects the charging supply to prevent overcharging.

Learning Outcomes

Learned comparator operation, relay switching, voltage adjustment, and practical circuit implementation.

Real-Life Application

Battery charging systems, rechargeable battery protection, and electronic charging circuits.
